No invitation? Bring your own bucket

folder-sync runs on any S3-compatible bucket you own. Sign a computer in with your own R2 key, and nobody else's bill is involved:

folder-sync login --key

Then folder-sync serve shows this same view over your own computer (it comes with the next release).

    Use it in your app

    Mount folder-sync at /fs/ on your app's domain, and add two lines:

    <script type="module" src="/fs/ui/v1/folder-sync.js"></script>
<folder-sync-status project="notes"></folder-sync-status>
